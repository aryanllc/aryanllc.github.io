How to Play

Control the snake around the board and eat the red food. Every food item increases your score and makes the snake longer. Avoid hitting the walls and your own body.

Your objective is simple: survive as long as possible and beat your highest score. Think ahead before moving toward food because every new section of your snake reduces the amount of free space available.

Winning Tips

  • Plan your route before chasing food.
  • Keep an escape route available.
  • Leave plenty of space for your tail.
  • Avoid unnecessary sharp turns.
  • Use large loops when the snake gets long.
  • Be careful near corners and walls.
  • Do not rush toward every food item.
  • Practice on Slow mode first.
  • Increase speed gradually.
  • Think one or two moves ahead.
  • Use Pause if you need time to plan.
  • Try to beat your own high score.

Controls

↑ / W — Move up

↓ / S — Move down

← / A — Move left

→ / D — Move right

Space / P — Pause

Enter — Restart after Game Over

On mobile, use the on-screen directional buttons.

Game Modes

Slow: Great for learning and planning.

Normal: Balanced gameplay.

Fast: Requires quicker reactions.

Extreme: A serious reflex challenge.

Arrow Keys / WASD on desktop. Use the controls on mobile. Press Space or P to pause.
